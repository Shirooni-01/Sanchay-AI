AI Product Analysis Interface & Decoupled Module

The AI Product Analysis engine is designed behind a clean, fully-typed service boundary (app/services/product_analysis_service.py and services/productAnalysis.py). It operates on a strict Read-Compute-Recommend architecture: extracting historical demand patterns from PostgreSQL sales and predicting sell-through probabilities before spoilage occurs.

• Zero Autonomous Mutations • Dual Human-in-the-Loop Approvals • Multi-Model Benchmarking (MAE/RMSE)

1. Multi-Horizon Demand Velocity

Trains exponential smoothing and regression models on weekday shopping patterns to forecast 7-day and 14-day demand with 90% confidence intervals.

2. FEFO Expiry Risk Scoring

Simulates FIFO/FEFO depletion curves against incoming shelf-life to compute probability of spoilage (0.0 to 1.0) and exact financial capital at risk.

3. Cross-Store Absorption Matching

Pairs surplus batches with nearby sister shops experiencing high velocity, balancing road distance against gross margin to maximize net salvage profits.
