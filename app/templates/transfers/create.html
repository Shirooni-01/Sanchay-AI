Dual-Approval Guarantee

How transfers work (BR-008, BR-009):

  1. Proposal: Submitting this form sends an offer to the recipient shop. No stock is moved yet.
  2. Mutual Agreement: Once the recipient owner approves, stock is locked into reserved_qty so you cannot accidentally sell it.
  3. Physical Handover: Your shop marks the units as DISPATCHED.
  4. Receipt & Completion: When the recipient owner confirms receipt, units are atomically deducted from your shop and added to theirs in a single ACID transaction.
